## Proposed limits and selection

- At most two unguided screens, ten minutes each, one attempt per task in fixed order. Stop at the first eligible failure; do not select the worst of several results.
- Eligibility requires a submitted, importable implementation that passes its basic happy path and reproducibly fails a predeclared behavioural requirement supported by the explicit request or pinned source. Mere noncompletion, timeout, setup failure, cosmetic issues or missing prose do not qualify.
- If a task qualifies, prepare one neutral guide (five minutes, at most 600 words), then run fresh ordinary and guided attempts (ten minutes each). Keep the guide author unaware of both tasks and the screening failure. Preserve empty or failed guide output and stop rather than retrying it.
- Maximum five sessions and 45 summed supervised session-minutes, excluding investigator preparation and evaluation. This is not a guaranteed token, credit or monetary cap. No retries, replacement tasks or extra reviewers.
- If neither task qualifies, stop and retain that result. Retain FC-01, all screened outcomes and costs. Any comparison remains conditional on prior unguided difficulty.

The proposed tasks involve multiple existing repository behaviours, which provides a reason to investigate guidance beyond an isolated helper. Neither task has been tested with an agent. Ten minutes may be easy or too restrictive for the larger scope; this is a proposed budget, not validated completion time. Both comparison arms get the same budget and disclosed requirements.
