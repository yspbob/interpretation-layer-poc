15 September 2026. Q06 is prepared and audited. No model has received its questions or answers.

## What is ready

The family contains six challenges for each of the three Phase 1 roles, giving 18 distinct items. These cover supported guidance, a valid alternative, an unsupported claim, a missed exception, missing information and evidence that cannot settle a claim.

The guidance assessor receives an empty guide in one challenge. Its expected answer must recognise that all four reference rules are missing. This complements Q05's partly complete guide. Other challenges test whether the reviewer distinguishes a library's behaviour from an unsupported assertion about earlier application processing.

The proposed role instructions and acceptance requirements remain unchanged. Source records and permitted inputs are separate from expected answers and investigator review notes.
