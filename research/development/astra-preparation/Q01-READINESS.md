# First qualification case prepared

This is the historical first case record. See [the completed bank record](BANK-READINESS.md) for the current private version, subsequent candidate metadata correction and next step.

On 13 September 2026, the user agreed to complete one qualification case before expanding the bank. Q01 is now prepared and checked. It has not been sent to a qualification model.

The case uses a family separate from the public development examples. Its documentation is available to the roles. This makes it a test of assessment quality within a stated scope; it does not test reconstruction of undocumented rules.

## What was checked

The expected answers are supported by pinned published sources and reproduced behaviour. Eight authored scenarios passed, four relevant upstream tests passed, and the checks detected three deliberate faults. These observations belong to one family, not fifteen independent cases.

The private case records scope, exceptions, valid alternatives, missing evidence and serious errors. It contains six challenges for each of three roles: the verifier, guidance assessor and assessor of verifier decisions. That is eighteen distinct role items, scheduled for two repetitions each. None of the thirty six planned calls has run.

All eighteen items passed checks for source identity, citation ranges, record format and permitted inputs. The scoring key remains separate from role requests. The verifier does not receive the assessment reference. The existing role prompts were preserved.

The first source pack exceeded the proposed input allowance. It was retained as a superseded preparation version. A second version uses exact excerpts with their original locations and retains the relevant counterevidence. Its largest encoded request is 28,053 bytes. With the existing development padding, it fits the proposed allowance. Actual provider token accounting remains unverified.

## What this does not establish

No verifier or assessor is qualified. Codex prepared and checked the case; there is no independent human certification. The source evidence and runtime observations justify the expected answers. An AI agreeing with them would not be additional ground truth.

This family does not cover every planned challenge. The remaining cases must include unresolved source conflicts and instructions embedded in candidate material. Full qualification also needs the final bank, agreed criteria and settings, a controlled batch runner, account checks and explicit spending authorisation.

The case and its answers remain outside public Git. They are stored on the preparation PC and are not transferred to the laptop by project sync.
