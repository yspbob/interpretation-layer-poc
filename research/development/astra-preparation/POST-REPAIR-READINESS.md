## Compatibility requires more than a format change

The old bank is correctly rejected by the current loader. The new bank uses contract `phase1-development-v0.2` and the already fixed `astra-assessment-instructions-v3`. No model instruction or runtime implementation changed during this preparation.

The conversion adds the supplied draft fingerprint for the verifier, updates its response schema, replaces repeated scope text in submitted reviews with a draft fingerprint, and recomputes submission identities for the assessor of verifier decisions. Guidance-assessor instructions and response schema remain unchanged. All 54 retained items preserve their source evidence, candidate claims, submitted verdicts and explanations. The removed scope fields matched the corresponding original claims; there was no hidden scope correction.

The reference review found an additional mismatch. Ten retained criteria allowed either rejection or an unresolved decision when evidence was absent. The repaired verifier explicitly requires **unresolved** in that situation. Those criteria now require that verdict for the verifier and its assessor. Four corresponding criteria in Q08 follow the same rule. The amendments are versioned before new answers and are based on the fixed instructions and evidence, not observed model performance. Guidance-assessor support labels are a separate vocabulary and retain their legitimate alternatives. Source-supported alternatives for genuinely conflicting requirements also remain.

The four qualification acceptance requirements, seriousness rules, six challenges per role and two repetitions are unchanged. Original keys and answers remain intact in their earlier freezes. A future result must identify the new criteria version rather than retroactively applying it to the stopped run.
