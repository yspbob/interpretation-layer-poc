The user found the paragraph about recovering preparation costs difficult to digest and asked for the same review across the document, followed by release of every pending change.

## What needed changing

Several paragraphs combined the procedure, its reason, its limitations and the current implementation status. Other passages used lists of abstract nouns where a reader needed to know who does what. The review table used different names from the comparison cards. The technical notes often assumed that readers already understood terms such as input packs, model contexts and containment.

## Changes applied

7. Technical explanations introduce the runner, virtual machine, container, input pack and file fingerprints. They separate execution, review, transfers, record keeping and limitations. The product table no longer ambiguously suggests that every listed product is still unselected when it means the four platform candidates.

The original technical claims, proposed tests and reasons remain available in an explicitly labelled disclosure. Source links, identifiers, families, classifications and the evidence download remain unchanged.
10. New explanatory copy avoids hyphens and dash based phrasing. Exact technical names, code, URLs and preserved original research records retain their necessary spelling. Paragraph spacing supports the additional separation without changing the playbook colours or fonts.
