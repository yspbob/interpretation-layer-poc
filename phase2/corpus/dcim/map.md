# dcim — subsystem map (NetBox @ ea4c205)

## What it is

`dcim` is the physical-infrastructure core of NetBox: 91 modules, 50,460 lines of code, 8 test modules, and no data files. It owns the site hierarchy (Region, SiteGroup, Site, Location), racks, device types and their component templates, devices and their components, modules, power distribution, cables, and the derived CablePath graph. [fg:subsystems:dcim]

It is also the hub of the whole codebase: 95 of its 206 inbound import edges target `dcim.models` alone, and every other domain app's production code (circuits, ipam, virtualization, wireless, vpn, extras) imports from it; tenancy does so only in its tests. [fg:imports:ipam.models.vlans->dcim.models] [fg:imports:virtualization.models.virtualmachines->dcim.models] [fg:imports:wireless.signals->dcim.models] [fg:imports:circuits.models.circuits->dcim.models] [fg:imports:vpn.filtersets->dcim.models] [fg:imports:extras.filtersets->dcim.models] [fg:imports:tenancy.tests.test_api->dcim.models]
