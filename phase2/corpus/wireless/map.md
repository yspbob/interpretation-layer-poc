# wireless — map (T0 ea4c205)

## What it is

`wireless` is a small Django app (38 modules, 3,252 code LOC, 4 test modules) that models three things: a hierarchy of `WirelessLANGroup`s, `WirelessLAN`s (SSIDs that interfaces join), and `WirelessLink`s (point-to-point connections between two wireless `dcim.Interface`s). It is a leaf domain that leans on `dcim` for interfaces, devices and location scoping, on `ipam` for the bridged VLAN, and on `tenancy` for ownership. ## Where change concentrates

Churn is highest in `models.py` (45 commits, 6 authors, last 2025-06-16), then the four form modules and `filtersets.py` (24-28 commits each), then `views.py` (22). Nearly all files date from the app's creation on 2021-10-12; the exception is the `api/serializers_/` package split (2024-02-27) and the GraphQL `filter_mixins.py` (2025-03-07), which are the newest structural additions.
